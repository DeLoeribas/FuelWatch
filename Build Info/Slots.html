Slots 2.1.1

New

  • Rest the pointer on a slot’s icon to see its window layout at a glance: every screen it uses, with each window and the app it belongs to.This works with the Window Layout thumbnail too, under Slot graphic in Settings ▸ General. Hover over a window in the preview to see its tabs, folders or project. Clicking the icon still lets you change it.

Improved

  • The numbered Desktop buttons at the bottom of the menu are now one Go to pop-up menu, so they fit however many Desktops you have.The pop-up shows the Desktop you’re on, and lists the slots kept to each Desktop so you know where you’re going. Pick another one to switch there; nothing is restored. With the menu open, Option-1, Option-2 and so on take you straight to that Desktop.

Fixed

  • Terminal windows now open in the folder they were in when you captured the slot, instead of your home folder.Slots you captured before this update saved your home folder. To fix one, open its Terminal windows in the right folders and choose Replace with Current Layout in the slot’s ••• menu.
